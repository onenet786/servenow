# ServeNow: Google Play store listing

This is ready-to-paste copy and an asset plan for the Play Console **Main store listing**, in English (en-US / en-GB) and Urdu (ur). It follows Google's listing rules: the title is 30 characters or fewer, the short description 80 or fewer and the full description 4,000 or fewer. It makes no ranking claims ("#1", "Best", "Popular"), no price or discount claims, and no comparisons with other apps. Sources and reasoning are in [`docs/research/2026-10-29-delivery-app-research.md`](../research/2026-10-29-delivery-app-research.md), section 4.

> **Check before publishing.** Every claim below must stay true:
> - Cash on delivery.
> - Order status tracking with rider call/WhatsApp.
> - Local stores in **Kot Addu**. Add other cities only once you deliver there.
> - The Urdu app interface.
>
> **Have a native Urdu speaker proofread the Urdu text.**

## 1. English listing

**App name (30 max):**
```
ServeNow: Food & Grocery
```
(24 characters)

**Short description (80 max):**
```
Order food & groceries in Kot Addu. Cash on delivery, track every order.
```
(72 characters)

**Full description (4,000 max):**
```
ServeNow brings your favourite Kot Addu restaurants, bakeries and grocery shops to your door. Order in a few taps and pay cash when your order arrives.

WHY SERVENOW
• Cash on delivery: pay the rider at your door. Paid orders show "Paid", so there is nothing extra to pay.

WHAT YOU CAN ORDER

• Grocery and daily needs: atta, doodh, sabzi, fruit and kiryana items.

2. Tap Add on the items you need, then View cart.
3. Confirm your address and place the order with cash on delivery.

ORDER AGAIN IN SECONDS
Your recent stores appear on the home screen so you can reorder quickly.

SUPPORT
Need help with an order? Open the order and tap Support to call or WhatsApp us.

ServeNow currently delivers in Kot Addu. More areas are coming soon.
```

(about 1,500 characters; enough room to add a city section later)

## 3. Keywords (there is no keyword field, so weave these in naturally)
Google indexes the title and the short and full descriptions. The copy above already uses most of these terms once or twice. Don't add keyword lists, because Google penalises that.
- **English:** food delivery, grocery delivery, order food, cash on delivery, Kot Addu, biryani, broast, pizza, burger, bakery, fruit, vegetables.
- **Roman Urdu** (not yet in the copy; test them in a custom listing or ads): khana order, ghar pe delivery, sauda salaf, kiryana, sabzi, doodh, atta.
- **Urdu:** کھانا، ڈیلیوری، گروسری، سودا سلف، کریانہ.
- **After launch:** check Play Console → Statistics → *Store listing acquisition → Search terms* every month, and adjust the short description towards the terms that convert.

## 6. Ratings and replies
- The app now asks for a Play review only after a customer's **2nd delivered order**, using Google's in-app review card. There's no button and no "Do you like the app?" question first, both of which Google forbids.
- Reply to every review within 24 hours, in the language it was written in. Templates:
  - **Good review:** "Thank you, [name]! We're glad your order arrived well. See you on your next order. Team ServeNow"
  - **Problem review:** "Sorry about this, [name]. Please WhatsApp us your order number at [support WhatsApp] so we can fix it today. Team ServeNow"
  - **Urdu:** "شکریہ [نام]! آپ کی رائے ہمارے لیے اہم ہے۔ مسئلے کی صورت میں آرڈر نمبر کے ساتھ ہمیں واٹس ایپ کریں۔ ٹیم سرو ناؤ"

## 7. Android vitals to watch after release
Play reduces a store listing's visibility when these go over Google's thresholds, measured over the last 28 days:
- **Crash rate:** at most 1.09% overall, and at most 8% on any single phone model.
- **ANR (frozen app) rate:** at most 0.47% overall.

Test on low-cost Tecno and Infinix phones, which are common in Pakistan, before each release. Check Play Console → *Android vitals* weekly for the first month.
